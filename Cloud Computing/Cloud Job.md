## 4. DevOps Engineer
---
- A DevOps Engineer bridges the gap between development and operations
- The role focuses on automation, collaboration, and CI/CD.
- The goal is to make software releases faster, more reliable and easier to manage.


#### Responsibilities.
---
- Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
- Automating infrastructure deployments.
- Managing containerized applications.
- Setting up monitoring and alerting systems.

This role focuses on automating the full dev-to-prod pipeline, beyond just cloud infrastructure

#### Skills Required
---
- Proficiency in scripting languages.
- Experience with containerization tools like docker.
- Knowledge of CI/CD platforms like Jenkins.
