# 📘 **LONDON SESSION TRADING SYSTEM (SOP)**

**Strategy: S&D + Liquidity Sweep + 1M Precision Entry**  
**Markets:** FX, Indices  
**Session:** London (07:00–12:00 London time)  
**Style:** Scalping / Intraday  
**Risk:** Funded-account compliant

---

# 🧩 **1. PRE-SESSION ROUTINE (15–20 minutes before London)**

### **1.1 Mark 15M Market Structure**

On the 15M chart, identify:

- Trend direction (Bullish/Bearish/Range)
    
- Last swing high + swing low
    
- Supply zones above price
    
- Demand zones below price
    

**Your only question here:**  
👉 _Where is price likely to go next to find liquidity?_

---

### **1.2 Identify Liquidity Pools**

Mark these levels:

- **Asian Highs & Lows**
    
- **Equal Highs / Equal Lows**
    
- **Double Tops / Double Bottoms**
    
- **Previous day high/low**
    
- **Internal 15M swing points**
    

You now know where London will hunt for liquidity.

---

### **1.3 Mark Killzones**

✔️ 07:00–08:00 → Pre-market manipulation  
✔️ 08:00–09:00 → London open sweep  
✔️ 09:00–10:30 → Real move of the day  
✔️ 11:00–12:00 → Slowdown / Reversals only

---

# 🧭 **2. 5M REFINEMENT PROCESS**

Once HTF (15M) is mapped, drop to 5M.

### On 5M you refine:

- The exact **supply/demand zone** price will react from
    
- Internal liquidity resting inside HTF zones
    
- Clean S&D flip zones
    
- Fair Value Gaps
    
- Micro Trend direction (bullish/bearish waves)
    

### The 5M job:

👉 **Tell you WHERE to enter, but not WHEN.**  
The “when” comes from the 1M.

---

# 🎯 **3. CONFIRMATION REQUIREMENTS BEFORE ANY ENTRY**

A valid setup MUST have:

### **A. Price must tap a 15M or 5M Zone**

No zone tap = No trade.

### **B. A Liquidity Event Must Occur**

Examples:

- Sweep of 15M high/low
    
- Stop hunt above equal highs/lows
    
- Sweep of Asian session high/low
    
- Breakout fake-out
    

### **C. Displacement Must Follow**

After liquidity sweep:

- A strong opposite candle
    
- An impulsive leg
    
- Market structure shift (MSS/CHoCH)
    

### **D. Entry Model on 1M MUST be present**

- Fair Value Gap (FVG)
    
- Order Block (OB)
    
- S&D Flip Zone
    
- Efficient/inefficient gap return
    

No confirmation?  
👉 **No trade.**

---

# ⚡ **4. 1M ENTRY MODEL (YOUR EXACT ENTRY PATTERN)**

### **Step-by-step:**

### **1️⃣ Price enters your 5M refined zone**

Either demand (for buys) or supply (for sells)

### **2️⃣ Price sweeps liquidity IN THAT ZONE**

Buy example:

- Sweeps low
    
- Creates low wick
    
- Traps sellers
    

Sell example:

- Sweeps high
    
- Creates high wick
    
- Traps buyers
    

### **3️⃣ Strong displacement candle prints**

This must:

- Break a minor 1M structure
    
- Show an imbalance (FVG)
    
- Close powerfully away from the sweep
    

### **4️⃣ Entry is on the FVG or OB return**

- Enter at first FVG retracement
    
- Or last bearish/bullish OB candle
    

### **5️⃣ Stop Loss**

Place SL at:

- The wick of the liquidity sweep
    
- Or behind the OB extreme
    

This gives **tight SL = big RR**.

### **6️⃣ Take Profit**

TP structure:

- TP1: 1R
    
- TP2: Liquidity pool
    
- TP3: Opposite zone
    
- TP4: If trending, let runner reach next HTF inefficiency
    

---

# 💼 **5. FUNDED ACCOUNT RISK MANAGEMENT RULES**

### **Daily Loss Rule: Max 1%**

If you lose 1% → stop trading.

### **Risk Per Trade: 0.25%–0.5%**

1M entries = tight stops = high RR even with small risk.

### **Maximum Trades Per Session: 1–3 Only**

More trades = more randomness.

### **Daily Target: 2–3%**

If you hit it → you’re done.

### **No Trading After Losing Streak**

- If 2 losses back-to-back → stop for the day.
    

### **Never Enter Without 1M Confirmation**

Funded accounts punish impulse.

---

# 📉 **6. TRADE FILTERING RULES (NO TRADE IF…)**

These save you from unnecessary losses.

❌ No trade without liquidity sweep  
❌ No trade far from a zone  
❌ No countertrend trades unless it’s a major HTF sweep  
❌ No trade during chop/consolidation  
❌ No trade minutes before high-impact news
